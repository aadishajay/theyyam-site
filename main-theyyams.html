Muthappan, Pottan and Vishnumoorthi: the main Theyyams

By [AUTHOR NAME] · Updated [DATE] · LGFC local guides

Hundreds of Theyyams exist, each with its own story. Three of the best known are Muthappan, Pottan and Vishnumoorthi. This is a short introduction, not a complete list.

Muthappan

Muthappan is among the most widely worshipped deities of the Kannur region, often described as a god of ordinary people who is close, approachable and generous. He is associated with the Parassinikkadavu shrine, and his worship has its own customs, including offerings that differ from many other temples.

Pottan

Pottan Theyyam retells the story of Shiva appearing in the guise of a lowly man to challenge the sage Adi Shankara, teaching that the divine does not belong to one caste. It is a dramatic form with a strong message about humility and equality.

Vishnumoorthi

Vishnumoorthi is linked to the Narasimha avatar of Vishnu, the half-man, half-lion who destroys a tyrant. It is a powerful, intense form, with a large headdress and a demanding performance.

Many more to know

Besides gods, Theyyam honours goddesses, heroes, ancestors and local spirits. Which forms you see depends on the shrine, and your guide will introduce them in context. Our North Malabar Deep Dive is built for travellers who want to see the variety.

Quick answers

How many Theyyams are there?

Hundreds of forms exist, each with its own story, costume and song.

Are all Theyyams gods?

No. Theyyam also honours goddesses, heroes, ancestors and local spirits.
