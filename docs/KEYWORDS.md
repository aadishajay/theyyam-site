# Keyword research: what people search for around Theyyam

Source: Google Trends, web search, past 5 years to 2026-10-18, captured 2026-10-19. Numbers are Trends' relative
index (100 = the most popular term in that list), **not search volume**. Trends hides low-volume terms, so a
missing term means "small", not "zero". No ranking tool was available; positions of competitors are not measured.

## 1. Who searches, and when
- **Where (worldwide):** India 100, UAE 62, Bahrain 54, Qatar 48, Oman 40; also Kuwait, Singapore, Saudi Arabia.
  That is Malayali and Indian interest, much of it from the Gulf diaspora, not European tourism.
- **Western markets** (UK, US) show up with enough data for related queries but at much smaller scale. Germany and
  France did not load usable data in this session; treat them as unmeasured.
- **Seasonality:** peaks Jan to Feb (index 100 on 22 Feb 2026), a second rise Oct to Nov when the season opens, low
  from mid-May to July. Publish and refresh before October; update in December.
- **Trend:** interest is about 2x higher than five years ago, with spikes after the Kantara film (Oct 2022).
- **Travel-intent phrases are tiny:** "theyyam tour" shows no data; "theyyam calendar", "theyyam festival" and
  "theyyam kannur" average 1 to 2 against 40 for "theyyam".

## 5. What this means
1. **We will not rank first for "theyyam", "theyyam kerala" or "theyyam dance" soon.** Wikipedia, Kerala Tourism, DTPC
   Kannur and news sites hold those. Winning is realistic only on specific long tails and in one or two clusters
   (below). No honest plan can promise number one on a head term.
2. **Book-ready traffic is small.** Searches that say "tour" barely register. The audience is mostly curious
   (what, meaning, face, images, dance, Kantara) and the Gulf and Indian Malayali market. Convert curiosity into
   enquiries with helpful articles that end in the guided-trip call to action.
3. **Language priority changes.** The data favours Malayalam (and Gulf and Indian English) ahead of German, French or
   Spanish. The European pages remain a bet; measure first.
4. **Dates are the highest-intent cluster, and we cannot publish them.** Capture it with "how to find Theyyam dates
   responsibly" and a season-by-month page, and by offering the guided trip. Decision for LGFC: whether to mention public
   daily rituals (for example the Parassinikadavu Muthappan temple) since that is a public shrine, not a
   private family event.

## 6. Content to build next (in order)
1. "Is Theyyam a dance? No, it is a ritual" (captures *theyyam dance* and teaches the principle).
2. "Kantara and Theyyam: what the film shows and what it does not" (Panjurli, Guliga, Bhoota Kola, with care).
3. Kannur and North Malabar guide (*theyyam kerala / kannur*): districts, bases, how to travel, season.
4. "How to find Theyyam dates the respectful way" and a month-by-month season page (no event details).
5. Theyyam face painting, makeup and attire meanings (*face, painting, art, drawing*).
6. Bhoota Kola vs Theyyam, plus glossary additions (bhoota kola, deyyam, neeliyar kottam).
7. Malayalam pages for the top three (what is Theyyam, etiquette, season), reviewed by a native writer.
8. Own photography with descriptive file names and alt text, to compete in image search.
