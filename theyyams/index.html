The Theyyams of North Malabar

Hundreds of forms are recorded, each with its own story, face and song. Start here to meet some of them. Search by name, by what they stand for, or in Malayalam.

Gods and guardians

Deities of the Shaiva and Vaishnava traditions, village guardians and protective spirits.

Muthappan

മുത്തപ്പൻ · Gods and guardians

The approachable grandfather god of Kannur, worshipped by people of every background.

Vishnumoorthi

വിഷ്ണുമൂർത്തി · Gods and guardians

A powerful form of Vishnu, remembered through the story of the devotee Palanthai Kannan.

Pottan

പൊട്ടൻ · Gods and guardians

The god who answered a scholar’s pride with a question about equality.

Gulikan

ഗുളികൻ · Gods and guardians

A fierce guardian linked to time, fate and Yama.

Kuttichathan

കുട്ടിച്ചാത്തൻ · Gods and guardians

A fiery child guardian said to be the son of Shiva and Parvati.

Poomaruthan

പൂമരുതൻ · Gods and guardians

The brotherly guardian who travelled with the goddess Poomala.

Theechamundi

തീച്ചാമുണ്ഡി · Gods and guardians

A Theyyam of devotion and fire, remembered through the story of Narasimha and Prahlada.

Vayanattu Kulavan

വയനാട്ടുകുലവൻ · Gods and guardians

A blind hunter god, honoured as the chief deity of many Thiyya families.

Bhairavan

ഭൈരവൻ · Gods and guardians

The fierce wandering form of Shiva, who begs with a skull.

Puli Daivam

പുലി ദൈവങ്ങൾ · Gods and guardians

The tiger deities of North Malabar, born to Shiva and Parvati in tiger form.

Panjuruli

പഞ്ചുരുളി · Gods and guardians

The wild boar deity of the Tulu coast and Kasaragod, and the Theyyam closest to “Varaha”.

Angakkaran

അങ്കക്കാരൻ · Gods and guardians

The Theyyam that stands for Lakshmana in a set of Ramayana-based Theyyams.

Daivathar

ദൈവത്താർ · Gods and guardians

A Vaishnava Theyyam that stands for Rama, and one of the two major Vishnu-linked forms.
ബപ്പൂരൻPhoto to come

Bappuran

ബപ്പൂരൻ · Gods and guardians

The Theyyam that stands for Hanuman in the Ramayana-based group.

Pulikandan

പുലികണ്ടൻ · Gods and guardians

Shiva in tiger form, father of the tiger deities.

Puliyoor Kannan

പുലിയൂർ കണ്ണൻ · Gods and guardians

A tiger deity, brother of Puliyoor Kali.

Pulimaruthan

പുലിമരുതൻ · Gods and guardians

A tiger deity, described as a son of Shiva and Parvati in tiger form.
മരപ്പുലിPhoto to come

Marapuli

മരപ്പുലി · Gods and guardians

A tiger deity of the Puli Daivam family.

Goddesses

The Bhagavathis and other mother goddesses, from gentle and graceful to fierce.

Muchilot Bhagavathi

മുച്ചിലോട്ട് ഭഗവതി · Goddesses

A learned woman who became a goddess, and one of the most graceful of all Theyyams.

Uchitta Bhagavathy

ഉച്ചിട്ട · Goddesses

A beloved goddess whom women pray to for safe childbirth.

Vasoorimala

വസൂരിമാല · Goddesses

The goddess associated with epidemic disease, worshipped for healing.

Arya Poonkani

ആര്യപൂങ്കന്നി · Goddesses

A goddess who crossed the sea and found a friend in a Muslim sailor.

Chamundi

ചാമുണ്ഡി · Goddesses

A fierce warrior goddess, honoured in several forms across the region.

Karimchamundi

കരിം ചാമുണ്ഡി · Goddesses

A fierce, dark form of the warrior goddess Chamundi.

Kurathi Amma

കുറത്തി അമ്മ · Goddesses

A protective mother goddess, loved by the women of the household.

Puthiya Bhagavathi

പുതിയ ഭഗവതി · Goddesses

A goddess said to have risen from a sacrificial fire, and the main deity of many Kannur shrines.

Madayil Chamundi

മടയിൽ ചാമുണ്ഡി · Goddesses

The Chamundi who followed the demons into the underworld.

Kundora Chamundi

കുണ്ടോർ ചാമുണ്ഡി · Goddesses

The Chamundi who fought the demon Darika, and a guardian of village and home.

Padarkulangara Bhagavathy

പാടാർകുളങ്ങര ഭഗവതി · Goddesses

A goddess linked with Puthiya Bhagavathi, with an unusual story of a divine calf.

Puliyoor Kali

പുലിയൂർ കാളി · Goddesses

The tigress goddess, daughter of the tiger pair, and the best known of the tiger deities.

Pulikarinkali

പുള്ളിക്കരിങ്കാളി · Goddesses

The tigress mother of the tiger deities, known as Valiya Thamburatti.
വീരകാളിPhoto to come

Veerakali

വീരകാളി · Goddesses

A fierce goddess who receives Puthiya Bhagavathi and stays at her side.
ചീരുമ്പ ഭഗവതിPhoto to come

Cheerumba Bhagavathi

ചീരുമ്പ ഭഗവതി · Goddesses

A goddess of disease and healing, regarded as the younger sister of Puthiya Bhagavathi.
രക്ത ചാമുണ്ഡിPhoto to come

Rakta Chamundi

രക്ത ചാമുണ്ഡി · Goddesses

The blood-red Chamundi who drank the blood of Raktabija.

Heroes and ancestors

Ordinary people, warriors and wronged women who are worshipped as divine after their death.

Kathivanoor Veeran

കതിവനൂർ വീരൻ · Heroes and ancestors

The warrior Mandappan Chekavar, remembered as a hero and honoured as a god.

Pulimaranja Thondachan

പുലിമറഞ്ഞ തൊണ്ടച്ചൻ · Heroes and ancestors

A gifted man who learned to become a leopard, and was wronged for it.

Kadangot Makkam

കടങ്കോട്ട് മാക്കം · Heroes and ancestors

A wronged sister and her children, who became deities after their death.

Kandanar Kelan

കണ്ടനാർ കേളൻ · Heroes and ancestors

A devoted farmer who was lost in a forest fire and restored to life as a deity.

Vishakandan

വിഷകണ്ഠൻ · Heroes and ancestors

A healer of snakebite, honoured after he was killed out of envy.
പോലീസ് തെയ്യംPhoto to come

Police Theyyam

പോലീസ് തെയ്യം · Heroes and ancestors

A guardian in a khaki uniform who keeps order beside the goddess Karinchamundi.

Shared traditions

Forms that tell of Hindu and Muslim traditions meeting in North Malabar.

Bappiriyan

ബാപ്പിരിയൻ · Shared traditions

A sailor who is believed to have been a Muslim, honoured in a Hindu ritual.

Mappila Theyyam

മാപ്പിള തെയ്യം · Shared traditions

Theyyams that honour Muslim figures, kept by Hindu and Muslim communities together.

Ummachi

ഉമ്മച്ചി · Shared traditions

A Muslim woman honoured as a goddess, who wears a headscarf and is sung to in Mappila songs.

Aali Theyyam

ആലി തെയ്യം · Shared traditions

A Mappila Theyyam whose figure is Ali, brought before the goddess Chamundi.

Kalanthan Mukri

കലന്തൻ മുക്രി · Shared traditions

A mosque officer who became one with the goddess Karinchamundi.
യോഗ്യർ അകമ്പടിPhoto to come

Yogyar Akampadi

യോഗ്യർ അകമ്പടി · Shared traditions

The accompanying Theyyam that becomes Ummachi at the end of its ritual.

How we group them

This grouping is ours, to help readers. Scholars use other divisions, and many Theyyams fit more than one. Stories also differ from shrine to shrine and teller to teller. We do not publish dates or places, because Theyyam is worship kept by families and communities. See our etiquette guide before you go. Unsure of a word such as ottakolam or kaliyattam?
