## Who is Yogyar Akampadi?

Yogyar Akampadi is a Theyyam that accompanies Ummachi, according to a Malayalam news report. It is a rare detail, and we found it in only one source.

## The story

According to the report, the Theyyam appears first and changes into Ummachi at the end of its ritual. We found no separate story for it.

::gallery

## What you will see

It is described with sandalwood and rice-powder paste, a braid decorated with flowers, a sacred thread and ornaments.

::gloss

## In the tradition

It forms part of the Ummachi ritual, in which Hindus and Muslims take part together.

Please follow your guide’s lead during this quiet ritual.
