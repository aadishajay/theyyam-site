## Who is Puliyoor Kali?

Puliyoor Kali is the daughter of the tiger deities in the Puli Daivam family and is a tigress goddess. She appears alone in many shrines. When she appears with her mother, the mother is called Valiya Thamburatti and she is Cheriya Thamburatti, and in some places she appears with her brother Puliyoor Kannan.

## The story

The stories say that Shiva and Parvati took the forms of a tiger and a tigress, and that the tigress gave birth to cubs in a cave beneath a rock. Puliyoor Kali was the daughter. The sources differ on the number and names of her brothers.

## What you will see

She is described as fierce, with angry postures, yet graceful in her steps, and she spins in place with her tall headdress. Photographs show a very large, round, tiered red headdress and an orange-red painted face. At one moment the sacred headdress is lowered to touch the ground in reverence.

::gloss

## In the tradition

Puliyoor Kali is kept by members of the Vannan community.

:::note
She is among the most intense of the tiger deities. Stay behind the boundary.
:::
