## Who is Cheerumba Bhagavathi?

Cheerumba Bhagavathi is regarded as the younger sister of Puthiya Bhagavathi. The spelling varies, and so do the stories, as we explain below.

## The story

One popular account says the Cheerumbas were created by Shiva from his third eye, and that instead of spreading happiness they spread pox, so Shiva called for a sacrificial rite from which Puthiya Bhagavathi emerged. Another page describes the Cheerumbas as Shiva’s daughters who were themselves struck by smallpox, and says Puthiya Bhagavathi agreed not to spread the disease they had sown. The accounts do not agree, and we found no scholarly source.

## In the tradition

Like Puthiya Bhagavathi, she is linked with smallpox and its cure.
