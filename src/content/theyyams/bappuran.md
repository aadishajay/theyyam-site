## Who is Bappuran?

Bappuran is the third of the linked Ramayana-based Theyyams described by Kerala Tourism, standing for Hanuman. In one telling, the sailor Bappiriyan is also linked to Hanuman, but he is a different Theyyam with a different story.

## The story

We have found no published legend specific to Bappuran beyond its place in this group. The three Theyyams are said to represent deities installed after a spark from Rama’s bow split in three.

Bappuran belongs to the small Vaishnava group of Theyyams.
