## Who is Poomaruthan?

Poomaruthan is a god of North Malabar who is worshipped together with the goddess Poomala. His name joins “poo”, a flower, and “maruthan”, wind.

## The story

Poomala was admiring a heavenly garden and wanted to pick flowers, but the guards stopped her. She asked for help from one of the guards, who was resting in a blossom in the form of wind. He helped, and became her brotherly friend. They later travelled together to Malanadu in a great wooden ship built by Vishwakarma and came ashore on the North Malabar coast, where they settled and are worshipped.

::gallery

## What you will see

Poomaruthan has a painted face with a black moustache and beard, and a very large wooden headdress. He carries a sword and a wooden shield, and turns the shield as he moves. All the materials for the attire and face paint come from natural sources.

::gloss

## In the tradition

Poomaruthan appears in the shrines of Poomala Bhagavathi, who herself does not appear as a Theyyam. In some practice, assistants gather and shout to provoke the Theyyam, who may tap them with his shield.

:::note
The shield and sword are part of the ritual. Do not stand within the assistants’ circle.
:::
