## Who is Marapuli?

Marapuli is named by Kerala Tourism as one of the tiger Theyyams, and a popular page lists him among the sons of the tiger pair. We found almost nothing else about him.

## The story

He belongs to the same family story as Puliyoor Kali, where Shiva and Parvati took tiger forms. We found no separate story.

## What you will see

We did not find a published description and have no photograph to show.

::gloss

## In the tradition

He is part of the Puli Daivam family.

Ask your guide which tiger deities appear at the shrine you visit.
