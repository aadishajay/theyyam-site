## Who is Madayil Chamundi?

Madayil Chamundi is one of the three well-known forms of Chamundi, with Rakta Chamundi and Kundora Chamundi. She is identified with Kali, who followed the demons she was fighting into the underworld.

## The story

In the story, Kali pursued the demons into the underworld to carry on the fight, and for this the form is called Patalamurti and Madayil Chamundi. A popular account also says Kalabhairavi is another name for Madayil Chamundi, and adds that after she appeared on earth and disturbed its peace, she was given refuge in a family home and a shrine, and a Theyyam is held to win her blessings.

## What you will see

Madayil Chamundi wears a very large, round, halo-like headdress edged with palm leaf and red cloth, with a face painted orange and black and rich ornaments.

::gloss

## In the tradition

She is among the fierce warrior goddesses worshipped in family and village shrines.

Fierce goddesses are honoured with some distance. Let your guide place you.
