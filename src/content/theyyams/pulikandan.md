## Who is Pulikandan?

Pulikandan is the tiger form of Shiva in the story of the Puli Daivam. He is described as a rare Theyyam. A popular source also lists Kandapuli as one of his sons. Sources do not agree on this, and we found no scholarly source.

## The story

In the story, Shiva and Parvati, passing through a forest, saw tigers, and took on the forms of the tiger Pulikandan and the tigress Pulikarinkali. Their children became the tiger deities.

::gallery

## What you will see

In photographs, Pulikandan and Pulikarinkali appear in pairs, in red attire with tall red headdresses and white beards.

::gloss

## In the tradition

He appears with Pulikarinkali, and in some shrines with their daughter.

:::note
These rituals are quick and close. Keep behind the boundary.
:::
