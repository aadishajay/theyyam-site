## Who is Kadangot Makkam?

Kadangot Makkam is a woman of the North Malabar region who is worshipped as a goddess after being wronged. Her Theyyam is also called Makkappothi, or “Makkam and her children”.

## The story

Makkam was the only sister of twelve warrior brothers and was loved by all. While the brothers were away at war, their wives, who disliked her, spread a false story about her. The brothers believed it and decided to kill her. On a journey with her two children, Makkam stopped to drink at a well. There her brothers killed her and the children and threw them into the well. A passer-by who had witnessed it was killed as well.

Later, she took the form of a fierce deity, and the family home was destroyed by fire. The local people became convinced of her innocence and began to worship her, her children and the man who died with them.

## What you will see

Makkam appears together with her children and the man who died with her, each in their own attire.

::gloss

## In the tradition

Families who wish for children sometimes sponsor a Makkam Theyyam. She also appears at the annual festivals of her shrines.

:::note
This is a story of grief as well as justice. A quiet and respectful manner is especially important.
:::
