## Who is Aali Theyyam?

Aali Theyyam is one of the best-known Mappila Theyyams. Wikipedia says it is kept by members of the Vannan community in the Kumbla area and is linked to the legend of a local family. Names such as Ali Chamundi are also used for it.

## The story

A koladhari told The Week that the figure is Ali Mappila, a powerful magician who frightened people and wronged women. The goddess Chamundi took the form of a beautiful woman bathing at a pond, and as Ali chased her, she persuaded him to take off his protective amulet. She overcame him, but spared his life, made him repent and gave him a place at the shrine. The koladhari believes that on the day the goddess changes him into Ali.

Another story, told about the Mappila Theyyam of Kalanthan Mukri, is related but different.

## What you will see

Mappila Theyyams usually have less ornament and headgear than the great Theyyams. Hindus and Muslims both pray to Aali with folded hands.

::gloss

## In the tradition

The kolam is inherited within a family. The koladhari in that account inherited it from his mother’s brother, and works in a shop on other days.

:::note
This Theyyam is one of the clearest examples of shared worship. Stand quietly.
:::
