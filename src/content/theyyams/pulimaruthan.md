## Who is Pulimaruthan?

Pulimaruthan is a rare tiger Theyyam. Kerala Tourism lists it, and one popular page describes it as a son of Shiva and Parvati in the form of a tiger and tigress. Pulimaranja Thondachan, a different tiger deity, is a separate figure.

## The story

The page repeats the family story. Shiva and Parvati, passing through a forest, took the forms of a tiger and tigress, and their cubs became tiger deities. The sources do not describe his own story, his attire or those who keep him.

## In the tradition

He is part of the Puli Daivam family and appears in the shrines where it is worshipped.
