## Who is Ummachi?

Ummachi is one of the best-known female Mappila Theyyams. Hindus and Muslims both take part in the ritual, and it is often given as an example of how this tradition crosses the lines of caste and religion.

## The story

The legend says that a Muslim woman came to a palace to pound rice. To check that the rice was cooked properly she tasted it. A steward saw this, accused her of stealing and struck her with the pestle, and she died. She was later honoured as a deity.

::gallery

## What you will see

Ummachi wears a headscarf that covers part of the face, which sets her apart from almost every other Theyyam. The koladhari sometimes sits on a chair during the ritual. In place of the usual invocation song, the singers use Mappila folk songs in the melodies called ishal, and the ritual includes pounding rice and storytelling. Devotees can offer a cloth, which is then used as the headscarf.

::gloss

## In the tradition

Another Theyyam, Yogyar Akampadi, appears before her, wearing sandalwood and rice-paste markings, a braid with flowers and a sacred thread. According to a Malayalam news report, it changes into Ummachi at the end of its ritual.

:::note
Ummachi’s ritual is quiet and personal. Please keep your voice low and follow your guide’s lead.
:::
