## Who is Puliyoor Kannan?

Puliyoor Kannan is one of the tiger deities in the Puli Daivam family. Popular sources name him as a son of the tiger pair, and a brother of Puliyoor Kali. In some places Puliyoor Kali is said to appear together with him only.

## The story

A popular page about the family says the tiger pair had four sons and a daughter, or five sons by some beliefs, and names him among them. We found no separate story for him.

::gallery

## What you will see

In photographs, Puliyoor Kannan wears bright red attire, a red face with a large moustache and a tall headdress. Some photographs show a white beard.

::gloss

## In the tradition

He appears with the other tiger deities in the shrines where the family is worshipped.

:::note
These rituals are lively and close. Stay behind the boundary.
:::
