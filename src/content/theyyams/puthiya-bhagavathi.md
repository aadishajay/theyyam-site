## Who is Puthiya Bhagavathi?

Puthiya Bhagavathi is a goddess who is the main deity of many shrines in the Kannur region. She is generally identified with Bhadrakali, and Cheerumba Bhagavathi is regarded as her younger sister.

## The story

According to Kerala Tourism’s account, she emerged from a sacrificial fire that was lit to cure Shiva of smallpox. In some tellings she is accompanied by the warrior figures Padakulangara Veeran and Veerakali.

::gallery

## What you will see

She wears a very large, fan-shaped headdress made of tender coconut leaf, edged in yellow-green, with a bright red centre, over a skirt of palm-leaf strips and a painted red face.

::gloss

## In the tradition

She appears at many shrines in the region, and her Theyyam is also associated with ritual lamps.

:::note
The headdress is wide and the koladhari turns often. Keep clear.
:::
