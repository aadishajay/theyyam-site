## Who is Pulikarinkali?

Pulikarinkali is the tigress form of Parvati in the story of the Puli Daivam. When she appears with her daughter Puliyoor Kali, she is called Valiya Thamburatti, the elder lady, and her daughter is Cheriya Thamburatti.

## The story

The stories say she gave birth in a cave beneath a rock to cubs that became tiger deities. The number of cubs differs between sources. We found only one popular source for the details.

::gallery

## What you will see

She appears in red attire with a tall headdress and a painted face. In some shrines she and her daughter appear side by side.

::gloss

## In the tradition

She appears with Pulikandan and with her daughter, and is understood as the mother of the group.

:::note
When two Theyyams appear side by side, give both space.
:::
