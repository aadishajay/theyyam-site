## Who is Padarkulangara Bhagavathy?

Padarkulangara is a figure who appears with Puthiya Bhagavathi, according to Kerala Tourism. Sources do not agree on the name. Some write Padarkulangara Bhagavathy and others Padakulangara Veeran, which is a hero rather than a goddess. We found very little published about it.

## The story

A folklore page tells that Puthiya Bhagavathi, on her travels, killed a traveller who had agreed to go with her. He was transformed into a divine calf and is honoured in the related Padarkulangara Theyyam. Please treat this as a single, unverified telling.

::gallery

## What you will see

In photographs, the figure wears a very tall, pointed, conical red headdress over red attire and a painted face.

::gloss

## In the tradition

Padarkulangara appears as a companion of Puthiya Bhagavathi in her shrines.

:::note
Ask your guide which companion Theyyams appear with Puthiya Bhagavathi at the shrine you visit.
:::
