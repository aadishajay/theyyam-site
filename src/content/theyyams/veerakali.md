## Who is Veerakali?

Veerakali is a fierce goddess who appears with Puthiya Bhagavathi, according to Kerala Tourism. Few sources describe her, and they differ.

## The story

One account says Veerakali received Puthiya Bhagavathi when she was on a rampage after killing demons on earth. Another says a goddess appeared from Parvati’s anger at Shiva’s attendants, and that this was Veerakali. A folklore page says Veerakali at first resisted a vengeful goddess, then submitted and offered her a place.
