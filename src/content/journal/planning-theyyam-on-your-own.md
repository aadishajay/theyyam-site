## Information lives locally

Rituals are organised by families, temple committees and villages. News travels by word of mouth and local notices, in Malayalam, and plans can shift close to the day. There is no single reliable source for a visitor.

## Nights, not afternoons

Many rituals begin in the evening and run through the night, with the main appearance late and the finish near dawn. Rest, food and transport need planning around that.

## Getting there

Shrines are often in villages down narrow roads with little signage, and local taxis may not know them. Parking and walking distances vary.

## Knowing what you see

Each Theyyam has its own story, song and gestures. Without context it is easy to miss what matters, and to step wrongly. A guide translates and introduces you to the people who host the ritual.
