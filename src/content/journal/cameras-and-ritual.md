## Why the rules exist

To the families and devotees, the deity is present in the koladhari for the length of the ritual. Cameras, flash and phones held up among those present can feel intrusive during worship, and some shrines restrict them for that reason.

## The rules vary

Some shrines allow photos at certain moments, some ban them during the whole ritual and some have no rule beyond common courtesy. Rules can change from year to year, so ask your guide at each place rather than assuming.

## What we suggest

- Ask your guide before you take out a phone.
- If you are told no, put it away and simply be present.
- Never use flash.
- Do not photograph people without asking.

Many travellers find they remember the evening more when they are not behind a screen.
