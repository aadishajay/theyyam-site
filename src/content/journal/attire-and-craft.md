## The face

The face is painted by hand in patterns specific to each Theyyam, using mineral and plant-based colours. Every line has meaning, and the design is passed down within families.

## The headdress

The tall headdress, called *mudi*, is built on a frame of wood and bamboo, and decorated with cloth, metal ornaments and flowers. Some are taller than the koladhari and heavy to carry.

## The skirt and body

Skirts are often made of strips of tender coconut leaf, with red cloth, wooden breastplates and ornaments layered over. The look differs from one Theyyam to the next.

## The people

Makers of the attire, musicians and the family that keeps the Theyyam each hold a part of the knowledge. This is craft learned by observing and doing over many years.
