What is Theyyam?

A living ritual of north Kerala where the person who takes the kolam, after hours of painting and dressing, is believed to become the deity. Hundreds of forms exist, each with its own attire, song and story, kept by the same families for generations.

Questions, answered

When is the Theyyam season?

The main season runs from late October to May. We suggest the best weeks when you enquire.

Can I just turn up on my own?

Some rituals are open, but many are family occasions and timings are hard to know. A local guide or a host who knows the family helps you in respectfully.

Is it suitable for children and elders?

Yes, with planning. Rituals run late and involve standing, so we adjust pace and seating.

Can I take photos or video?

Not always. Many shrines ban photography and video, some during the whole ritual. Your guide tells you the rules at each place, and we suggest you simply be present.
